Our Story

A sanctuary
above the city

"There's something about sitting beside the lake, watching Tbilisi breathe, that makes every meal taste a little better."
— The Café Turtle Lake Team

Nestled near the famous Turtle Lake in the hills above Tbilisi, our café is a place to slow down. We serve honest, satisfying food — from freshly brewed Georgian coffee to hearty mains — in one of the city's most beloved natural settings.
